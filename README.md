# Data Analysis: Eniac – Magist Partnership Evaluation

This project evaluates a potential strategic partnership between **Eniac**, a high-end tech retailer, and **Magist**, a Brazilian marketplace and logistics provider. The analysis focuses on product catalog compatibility, pricing structures, and delivery performance to determine if Magist meets Eniac’s operational standards.

## 📋 Executive Summary

Based on the data analysis, the recommendation is **NO PARTNERSHIP**.

While Magist has a strong presence in high-HDI (Human Development Index) Brazilian states, their current business model is optimised for low-cost, non-tech products, and their logistics do not align with the expectations of high-end tech consumers. 

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 1. Product Mismatch

Magist's marketplace is heavily dominated by non-technical goods, creating a significant catalog misalignment with Eniac's specialised tech inventory. 

* 
**Tech Products on Magist:** Only 14% of total sales. 


* 
**Non-Tech Products on Magist:** 86% of total sales. 



### 2. Pricing Disparity

There is a massive gap between the average price of items currently sold on Magist and Eniac’s premium product line. 

* 
**Magist Tech Average Price:** 120€. 


* 
**Eniac Average Price:** 540€. 



### 3. Logistics & Delivery Performance

Magist's delivery times significantly exceed the industry standard in Brazil (12 days) and fail to meet Eniac’s internal requirements. 

* 
**Actual Delivery Time:** 24 days. 


* 
**Estimated Delivery Time:** 13 days. 


* 
**The Gap:** There is a large discrepancy between estimated and actual delivery dates, posing a high risk to customer satisfaction. 



### 4. Market Reach (HDI Analysis)

Analysis shows that **96% of Magist’s orders** originate from customers in the Top 10 Brazilian states by Human Development Index (HDI), such as São Paulo (SP) and Rio de Janeiro (RJ). While this indicates a high-value customer base, the logistical failures outweigh the geographical benefits.
